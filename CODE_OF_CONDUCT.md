# 🌈 Code of conduct

These projects are open to everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

💚 **Expected**: being kind and patient, assuming good faith, giving and accepting criticism of the work, not of the
person, and helping newcomers.

🚫 **Not accepted**: insults, harassment, discriminatory remarks, unwanted sexual attention, publishing someone's
private information, and repeated disruption of discussions.

📮 **Reporting**: write to vincent.foriel@gmail.com. Reports are read by the maintainer only and kept confidential.

⚖️ **Consequences**: depending on the case, a reminder of these rules, the removal of comments, or a block from the
repositories.
